Practical AI for Management

From occasional AI use to a repeatable way of working for management.

This is not generic prompt training, a catalogue of tools, or a presentation about the “future of AI”. It is a practical workshop built around real management work.

The problem

Access to AI exists. A shared practice is still being built.

The workshop starts from situations where managers already use AI, but each person still builds their own context, instructions, verification and working method.

Inconsistent use
Each manager invents their own way of working.

ChatGPT, Copilot, Claude or other platforms are used differently from person to person.

Rebuilt context
Every conversation starts almost from scratch.

Project context, sources and useful instructions are not organized for reuse.

Verification & confidentiality
There are legitimate questions about what can be trusted and what can be shared.

We introduce practical principles for verification, sources, responsible use and confidentiality.

Prioritization
Many AI ideas. Few clear choices.

We separate what is worth using now, what is worth testing, and what should not be prioritized.

Maturity model

Beyond the prompt: context, sources, reuse — then automation.

The principles are vendor-neutral. Terminology and capabilities differ across ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Claude and other platforms; we do not assume feature parity across products.
