WHEN IT HELPS

Access to AI does not automatically create productive adoption.

The programme fits teams that have tools but not yet a shared way of working.

Inconsistent use

People experiment independently and produce results that are hard to compare or reuse.

Rebuilding context

Sources, instructions and relevant information must be assembled again for every task.

Trust and responsibility

The team needs clarity on appropriate information use, verification and where human accountability remains.

Too many possibilities

There are plenty of possible use cases, but limited prioritization and few practical examples.

WHAT WE PRACTISE

AI applied to people's work, not a list of features.

Scenarios are chosen with the sponsor based on roles and available tools.

Documents, research and synthesis

Using documents and sources to extract, compare and summarize relevant information.

Meetings and decisions

Preparing questions, materials and information before meetings and decisions.

Reusable working practices

Project context, instructions, relevant sources and checks that the team can use again.

PROGRAMME FORMAT

A practical workshop tailored to the team.

The customized programme aims to build practical capability and clear next steps, not transform an entire organization in one day.

  1. 01 · Sponsor conversation · 45–60 min

    Identify roles, recurring work, existing tools and appropriate examples.

  2. 02 · Practical workshop · 1 day

    Work on relevant scenarios involving context, documents, verification, confidentiality and responsible use.

  3. 03 · AI Adoption Map

    Prioritize 3–5 use cases: use now, test or do not prioritize.

  4. 04 · Follow-up and opportunities

    The customized programme includes a follow-up session. Separately identify workflows that may justify an automation pilot.

WHAT THE TEAM TAKES AWAY
